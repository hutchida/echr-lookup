## Why (Dan's feedback after testing)
Asked "Does the Convention stop the UK deporting criminals?", the app returns a list of cited facts with no direct answer. Dan expected "No/Not as a rule, because …" followed by the evidence. Three causes:
1. **Prompt shape.** `py/rag/answer/generate.py`'s `SYSTEM_PROMPT` asks only for 3–6 single-paragraph claims and over-applies neutrality, so the model never states the bottom line.
2. **Weak evidence.** The answer opened with domestic statute text (Immigration Act 1971 s.3(5)(a), from A.H. Khan §19) and used Soering (extradition) for Article 3, while stronger deportation cases in the corpus (e.g. Chahal, Othman (Abu Qatada)) were not retrieved into the context. The reranker tends to lift statute-quoting passages (visible in `/lab`).
3. **Verifier bug.** `py/rag/answer/verify.py` calls the verify model with `max_tokens=200` and no retry; replies for multi-paragraph claims get cut off, `parse_json_object` raises `JSONDecodeError`, and the claim is shown as "Unverified: Verification failed (JSONDecodeError)".

**Dan's decision (binding):** a direct answer about **what the law does** is neutral and wanted. The system still never says whether a law, policy or judgment is good or bad, and never whether the UK should stay in or leave the Convention. Value questions ("Should the UK leave the ECHR?") keep today's behaviour: no verdict, the strongest sourced points on each side.

## Change
### Contract (`packages/contract`; you own this change, nothing else may change)
Add two **optional** fields to `Claim` (backward compatible: stored eval runs and old fixtures must still validate):
- `role`: `z.enum(["short_answer", "point"]).optional()`; absent means `"point"`. At most one `short_answer`, and it is the first claim.
- `group`: `z.string().max(80).optional()`: a short, neutral heading shared by consecutive points (e.g. "Where the Court found a deportation would breach the Convention" / "Where the Court allowed the deportation").
Re-export the JSON Schema (`pnpm --filter @echr/contract export`), add or extend a fixture showing both fields, keep the contract tests green. Python validates against the exported schema, so update its tests too.

### Answer pipeline (`py/rag/answer/**`)
- **Answer first.** For explain questions (not value questions, not stats), the generator returns a `short_answer` claim first: one plain sentence that answers the question directly ("Not as a general rule: …"), stating what the Court has done, citing 1–3 paragraphs that **together** support it. Then 3–6 points, grouped with `group` headings when the question has two sides or several grounds. `answer_text` should read naturally top to bottom.
- **Verify the short answer** against its cited paragraphs together (it may summarise them; it must not add anything they don't support). If it's unsupported, it's flagged like any other claim.
- **Better evidence, generically.** Improve what reaches the generator for "does X stop/allow/require Y"-type legal questions, e.g. extend the query planning in `plan.py` (today only value questions get it) so the context covers the legal tests or grounds and outcomes both ways; and/or diversify the context (cap chunks per document; don't let statute text crowd out the Court's reasoning and operative provisions). Measure what you change: show before/after context for demo Q1 and Q2 in the PR. Keep `/ask` < 15 s.
- **Verifier fix:** enough `max_tokens` for the reply, one retry on a parse failure, and a test that a truncated first reply is retried.

### Web (`apps/web/components/**`, `apps/web/app/ask/**`)
Render the short answer prominently ("Short answer" label, larger text, its chips and badge), then the points under their `group` headings; answers without these fields render exactly as today (old fixtures). Update the unit tests.
