# W0: landing the refactor A–G

## 1. What this is, in plain English

After Dan's second review of the demo, the previous orchestrator made a set of honesty and clarity fixes, called **A–G**. They sat on a branch (`wip/refactor-a-g`) and were never reviewed or merged. W0 is the **gate** (a checkpoint every later task waits for) that lands them: review each fix against its claim, make sure the checks are green, and merge. The MCP tasks that follow (M1–M3, which expose retrieval as tools for Claude) build on this code, so they must start from a reviewed `main`.

The fixes, in words:

- **A**: the Ask page says what it searches (how many documents, UK cases only, a sample or not), from `GET /stats`. A refusal no longer reads as "the Court never decided this".
- **B**: "How many X, and how many of them Y?" reports both numbers (`scope_total` is the number before the outcome filter), and every filter in the SQL is named in plain words.
- **C**: a count's example cases quote the clause that states the counted outcome, or no quote at all. The examples are illustrations, never the count's citations.
- **D**: every `Citation` has a `doc_type`, and chips and sources show it, so a *legal summary* (the Court's staff describing a judgment) is visibly not the judgment.
- **E**: quotes come from `best_quote`: verbatim, whole sentences, containing the supporting sentence; an ellipsis marks a cut.
- **F**: **reach of the holding**: the short answer may not open with a bare "Yes" or "No" unless a cited paragraph states the general rule.
- **G**: two or three **follow-up** links per answer, each a fresh stateless `GET /ask?q=…`.

## 2. Why it matters

Take "How many Grand Chamber judgments are there, and how many of them found a violation?". Before A–G the answer gave one number and listed example quotes such as "Holds that there has been no violation", directly under a count of violations. A reader who clicked a quote found the opposite of what the number claimed. After A–G: "The indexed corpus has 10 Grand Chamber judgments; 9 of them found at least one violation.", with the one case that has no violation clause listed without a quote. This is the kind of defect that makes someone distrust every other number.

## 3. How it works here

```
GET /stats ──────────────► apps/web  corpus-scope.tsx        (A: what is searched)
question ─► stats route ─► py/rag/answer/stats.py
              │              wants_total_and_subset ─► 2 SQL runs ─► scope_total        (B)
              │              _scope_phrase: every filter in words                       (B)
              │              pick_operative_clause ─► example quote or none             (C)
              └► Answer.citations / matching_cases (doc_type)  ─► citation-chip.tsx     (D)
question ─► rag route ─► citations.best_quote (sentence windows)                        (E)
                         generate.py ANSWER_FIRST_RULES (reach of the holding)          (F)
Answer ──► follow-ups.tsx ─► links to /ask?q=…                                          (G)
```

W0 itself changes no behaviour. It reviewed the 26 files, ran the checks, refreshed `docs/learn/S4.md` and `docs/learn/I1.md`, and checked that retrieval did not move (ranking code was not touched, so recall and MRR must equal the last stored run). `packages/contract/schema/*.json` is generated from the zod source (`pnpm contract:export`), and must show no diff.

## 5. Limits / what can go wrong

- **F is a prompt rule.** The tests check that the rule text is in the prompts; nothing checks the real model's openings. Only a judged (paid) eval would, and W0 makes no live calls.
- **B triggers on wording.** A two-part question phrased unusually gets one number.
- **E is heuristic**, with a short abbreviation list; an unlisted abbreviation can split a sentence.
- **The stats answer's `citations` are examples.** They are listed so the page can show quotes, but no claim cites them (`citation_ids` is empty); the SQL is the evidence.
- **Eval doc generator.** `rag.eval --retrieval-only` overwrites the generated `docs/eval-results.md`, which loses the answer-level rows from the last judged run. That is why W0 did not commit the refreshed file.
